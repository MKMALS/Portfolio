Our Heritage & Tradition

Our journey began with a simple mission: to revive the rich taste and health benefits of karuppatti and bring it directly to your home. For generations, palm jaggery has been a staple in traditional households, valued not just for its distinctive taste but also for its numerous health benefits.

At Karuppatti Kadai, we work directly with skilled artisans who have been crafting palm jaggery for decades using time-honored methods. Each batch is carefully prepared to ensure the highest quality and authentic taste that connects you to our cultural roots.
